The Enchanted Block Tower

THE WITCH'S TOWER · MIDNIGHT

HOW TO PLAY

  1. There are five floors. Each one hides 6 treasures.
  2. ✦ Golden sparkles mark a chest, a book, a cauldron or a mirror with a treasure inside. Press E (on a tablet, tap USE) and answer the question.
  3. You get two tries. Getting it right first time scores a point.
  4. With 6 treasures, the door opens and you can go down the stairs. At the bottom, beat Hexabel in a word duel to escape!
